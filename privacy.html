Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Short version: this website does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. There are no accounts, no forms and no ads on this site.

Who we are

Fractured SMP is a community-run Minecraft survival multiplayer server. This website introduces the server, its creators and its videos. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means the people who run Fractured SMP.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information through this website. You can browse every page without signing up or typing anything in.

Services this site uses

A few outside companies help the site work. When your browser loads the page, these companies receive basic technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the page you visited. Each one handles that under its own privacy policy:

  • Hosting: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts: text styles load from Google Fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Video thumbnails: preview images for YouTube videos load from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), which are run by Google under the same policy.

Links to Discord and YouTube

Buttons on this site open Discord and YouTube. Once you leave this site, those services' own privacy policies apply, not this one:

  • Discord Privacy Policy
  • YouTube (Google) Privacy Policy

Joining the server happens on Discord. Anything you share there, including applications, messages and your username, is handled by Discord and the server's moderators, not by this website.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies or use tracking or advertising tools.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Discord requires users to be at least 13 (older in some countries). If you are a parent and have a question, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, for example by adding a form or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the staff in our Discord server.
